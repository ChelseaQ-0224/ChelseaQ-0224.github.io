### Research interests
- Algorithmic visibility in platform society
- Data and affective communication
- Marginalized experiences in digital environments

### Currently
- Based in: China / Australia  
- Affiliation: Monash University

### Selected themes I’m exploring
- How recommendation and ranking systems shape attention and public discourse
- How emotions circulate as data and become “measurable” signals
- How people at the margins develop strategies of expression and visibility
