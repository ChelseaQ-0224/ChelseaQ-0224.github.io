Hi, I’m Qianshi Qi. I like tracing the algorithmic spotlight—what it illuminates, what it amplifies, and what it leaves in the shadows. My research interests include algorithmic visibility in platform society, data and affective communication, and marginalized experiences in digital environments.

Outside academia, I’m a fan of creative cocktails (especially whisky-based ones), and I enjoy exploring cities, museums, and everyday stories worth documenting. If you’d like to chat about research, projects, or simply swap good reading recommendations, feel free to reach out.
